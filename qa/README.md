# QA kit for the testing agent

1. Open Antigravity on your laptop, in any folder.
2. Paste the whole of `qa/AGENT_PROMPT.md` as the task (or give it this link: https://github.com/pranjalkumaraug2003-ops/CookBookAI/blob/main/qa/AGENT_PROMPT.md).
3. Stay nearby for about 30 minutes: it will ask you to stand at 2, 3 and 5 ft, make gestures, say commands and rate voices. Turn the laptop volume up.
4. When it finishes, attach `qa-run\qa\out\qa-results.zip` to the Claude chat. It contains no video or audio of you.
